Mission & Vision

Kijitonyama Youth Choir of Kijitonyama Lutheran Church serves Dar es Salaam and Tanzania through sacred music, youth discipleship, and community outreach.

Our Mission

Bringing Young People to Christ Through Song

We proclaim the Gospel and bring young people to Jesus through choral music in churches, colleges, schools, and community gatherings across Dar es Salaam and beyond.

Our Vision

Glorifying God & Touching Lives Across East Africa

We strive to be a leading youth choir in East Africa—uniting spiritual devotion with musical excellence so that every rehearsal, service, and concert uplifts hearts.

Churches & Schools

Leading worship services and visiting schools and colleges with Gospel songs.

Youth Discipleship

Nurturing young men and women in Christian faith, character, and fellowship.

Musical Training

Coaching singers in sight-reading, vocal technique, and four-part harmony.

Worship & Fellowship

Gathering every Tuesday and Thursday from 6:00 PM to 8:30 PM at Kijitonyama.

Community Service

Supporting orphanages, hospitals, and neighbors in need through charity visits.

Servant Leadership

Equipping youth leaders to serve the church and community with integrity.

Prayer & Discipline

Anchoring every rehearsal and ministry trip in prayer, unity, and commitment.

Open Welcome

Welcoming new voices across soprano, alto, tenor, and bass sections.
