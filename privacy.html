Privacy & Your Information

  • What information do we collect?

    Membership applications may include your name, date of birth, email, phone, area of residence, preferred voice part and information you choose to share. Contact forms collect your name, email, phone and message.

  • Why do we collect it?

    We use information to process membership applications, answer questions, arrange first rehearsals and send rehearsal or concert information you request.

  • How do we protect your information?

    We protect information using access-controlled systems and secure connections. Access is limited to choir staff who need it for their responsibilities.

  • Who can see your information?

    We do not share personal information without your consent, except where the law requires it.

  • What are your privacy rights?

    You may ask to access, correct or delete your information. Contact the choir using the details on our Contact page.

  • How long do we keep information?

    We keep information only for as long as needed to respond to you or manage membership and choir communications.

  • How can you contact the choir?

    Contact Kijitonyama Youth Choir at Kijitonyama Lutheran Church, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, or call +255 753 423 251.

  • When is this notice updated?

    We may update this notice from time to time. Last updated: 21 October 2026.

  • What about event photographs?

    We publish names and event photographs only with permission or at a member’s request.
