Joining the Choir

  • Who can join the choir?

    Young people who want to sing, grow in faith and serve through music are welcome to ask about joining.

  • Do I need to read music?

    No. The choir helps members develop sight-reading and vocal skills through practice and coaching.

  • When do rehearsals take place?

    Rehearsals are Tuesday and Thursday, 6:00 PM–8:30 PM at Kijitonyama Lutheran Church in Dar es Salaam.

  • How do I get started?

    Come to a rehearsal or use the Contact page to ask the choir about membership.

  • Can I support the choir as a patron?

    Yes. Patrons can support choir activities and community outreach.

  • Where does the choir rehearse?

    At Kijitonyama Lutheran Church, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.

  • What should I bring to my first rehearsal?

    Bring yourself and an interest in singing. The choir can explain any next steps at rehearsal.

  • How can I ask about membership?

    Call +255 753 423 251 or use the Contact page.

  • How much does it cost to join?

    Contact the choir for current membership information and any applicable costs.
