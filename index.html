Our Conviction

Sing unto the Lord a new song.

Called to serve through music.

We are the youth choir of Kijitonyama Lutheran Church, serving churches and schools across Dar es Salaam and Tanzania. Our singers build musical skill, faith and friendship through shared worship and disciplined rehearsal.
